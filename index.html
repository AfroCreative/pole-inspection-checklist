What counts as Tier 1?
  • Conductor down, floating off the insulator, or hanging low
  • Broken or disconnected neutral
  • Fire, smoke, active arcing or fresh burn damage
  • Pole broken, cracked through or leaning badly
  • Transformer, recloser or capacitor tank bulging or ruptured
  • Limb on a primary line, or vines reaching live parts
  • Thermally hot: over 15 °C hotter than other phases, or over 40 °C above ambient
  • Anything that could fall or put the public at risk

When it applies, tick “Tier 1 — needs immediate attention” in the defect tool. Always follow your company's escalation procedure.
