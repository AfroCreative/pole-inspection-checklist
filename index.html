Tier 1 – report now

    Escalate these before moving to the next pole.
    What counts as Tier 1?
    • Conductor down, off the insulator, or hanging low
    • Broken or disconnected neutral
    • Fire, smoke, active arcing or fresh burn damage
    • Pole broken, cracked through or leaning badly
    • Transformer, recloser or capacitor tank bulging or ruptured
    • Tree or object in contact with primary wires
    • Cross arm broken or burnt with wires still on it
    • Anything that could fall or put the public at risk

    Critical faults are flagged Tier 1 automatically. Tick “Tier 1” on any other fault you think is urgent. Always follow your company's escalation procedure.

    Work top to bottom. Every item needs OK or Fault before a section turns green. If a component isn't on the pole, tick “Not on this pole” so it's skipped on purpose. Tap a figure name to see what the equipment looks like.
